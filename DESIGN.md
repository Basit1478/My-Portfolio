# Editorial Portfolio Design
## World
A contemporary design monograph translated to the web: paper-white fields, ink typography, architectural light, hairline rules, and sparing vermilion signals.
## Type
Prata carries expressive editorial display copy; Manrope handles body text and compact notation.
## Composition
A fixed desktop navigation rail anchors an asymmetrical split hero. Sections share rules rather than containers. Mobile removes the rail and preserves the reading hierarchy in one column.
Project previews follow a consistent landscape ledger: every live capture uses a 16:10 frame across desktop and mobile, while its height remains responsive to its width.
## Color
Paper #faf9f6, ink #11110f, muted #696862, rule #deddd7, signal #ff5a1f.
## Motion
GSAP orchestrates the opening and restrained scroll reveals with 16px travel and exponential ease-out. Reduced-motion disables movement.
